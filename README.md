# United States Emissions Breakdown — 2023

An interactive data analytics dashboard built in Databricks to explore and visualize United States emissions data for 2023.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes emissions data across the United States through geographic visualization, population comparisons, state-level emission percentages, and county-level emission totals. The dashboard helps users explore emission distribution and identify patterns in the available dataset.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Geographic Emissions Map
- Visualizes the geographic distribution of emissions across the continental United States.
- Helps identify geographic concentrations and regional patterns.

### 2. Emissions vs. Population
- Uses a scatter plot to compare emissions per person with population.
- Helps explore relationships, patterns, and potential outliers in the data.

### 3. Emission Percentage by State
- Uses a donut chart to display the percentage contribution of selected states.
- Helps compare the relative shares represented in the dataset.

### 4. Total Emissions by County
- Uses a bar chart to compare total emissions across selected counties.
- Helps identify counties with higher reported emission totals.

## Limitations

- Findings depend on the coverage, quality, and definitions of the source dataset.
- Emissions per person and total emissions are different measures and should not be interpreted interchangeably.
- Observed relationships do not establish causation.
